How the implied value is derived

The snapshot records a market cap for of its protocols. Those are the comparables. The model regresses their log market cap on log TVL, category, seven-day TVL growth and tokenless age (a least-squares size line, then shrunk category medians and growth and age slopes fitted on the residuals, three passes; the growth and age slopes are capped and shrunk, so one hot week cannot price a protocol). A protocol that records no token is priced by that fitted relationship at its own figures: curve = exp(a + b·ln TVL) × category multiple × growth multiple × age multiple, and the final price is a geometric blend — 65% that curve, 35% the price at its category's own observed median cap/TVL — with the multiple kept inside the range the comparables themselves trade in. The blend is what the holdout below supports. Nothing is priced from a name.

Fitted on comparables (recorded cap of $1M or more): TVL slope , growth coefficient , age coefficient , residual σ in log terms — each row's band is its implied value × e±σ. Tested out of sample on a deterministic half of the comparables (fit on even slug hashes, scored on odd): median log error , of test caps landed within 2× of the model's price. The production fit uses every comparable.

The board ranks only protocols that record no token (no market cap, no CoinGecko id, no symbol) and pass the watchlist's own rule applied at every size, not only above the floor: not a bridge, staking or exchange product (those categories are wrappers over an asset that already trades), not an exchange-named protocol, not a versioned pool, not a wrapped or staked product by name — rows, the whole -protocol watchlist among them. Protocols whose token already trades are never ranked: the largest are shown apart below, at the market's own values. A further protocols record a token but no cap, and are set aside as already tokenised. Each row's plan is generated from its own record — its category's mechanics, its primary chain's gas costs, a position sized to its TVL tier, a length set by its tokenless age — and netted out in its last step.
